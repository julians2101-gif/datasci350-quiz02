Also calculated the average IMDb rating by decade.

Python code used:

films["decade"] = films["year"] // 10 * 10

decade_ratings = (
    films.groupby("decade")["imdb_rating"]
    .mean()
    .round(2)
)

decade_ratings.to_frame(name="Mean IMDb Rating")

Explanation:
The decade calculation groups release years into
decades. The groupby operation calculates the mean
IMDb rating for films in each decade.
